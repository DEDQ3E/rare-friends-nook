# Rare Friends: Friend Nook

An isometric life sim starring your Rare Friend. Its family decides its temperament, its generation how strong
that character is. See `DESIGN.md` in the repository root.

- Click furniture to choose an action, click the floor to walk, arrows / WASD to walk, E to use, F to talk.
- Gift Box (1 RF, simulated) opens a keepsake of five rarity tiers; keepsakes can be kept or sold back.
- RF, purchases and rewards are simulated in this preview. A reload starts a fresh session.
